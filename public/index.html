Company Research Portal

Identify five companies that hire, or are likely to hire, freshers in your field of study. Each company may be submitted by one student only.

How to fill this form (please read once)
Domain

The job role or area you would apply for in that company. Example: if you want to join Celebal Technologies as a data engineer, your domain is Data Engineer. Pick it from the list. If your role is not listed, choose Other and type it.

Domain-specific link or email

Where you can apply for that exact role in that company. Give either a link to the opening or careers page for that role, or the email of the HR or the person who handles hiring for it. Example: the page listing the Data Engineer opening, or the HR email for data roles.

General fresher hiring link or email

The common way any fresher can apply to that company, whatever the role. Many companies, like TCS, do not hire for one particular role. They visit colleges and ask for freshers with a given skill set. Give the fresher or campus hiring page, or the email where freshers can apply.

Company hires for specific rolesChoose your role as the domain. Give the domain-specific link or email. The general fresher link or email is optional, add it if you have it.
Company hires freshers by skills, not rolesChoose General (not domain specific) as the domain. Give only the general fresher hiring link or email.
  • Give at least one of the two link or email fields. You may give both.
  • Copy links and emails from the company's official website or careers page. Do not make them up. Make sure the link opens.
  • The company's name and website are checked live. A company that is already taken, or only slightly different in spelling, is not accepted. Watch the dropdown while you type the name.
  • It is first come, first served, and you can submit only once. Entries are final.
Please note: entries are final and these details will be recorded for reference. Provide accurate details only.
