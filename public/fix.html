Fix your companies

Your five companies stay reserved for you. Fill in the three details for every company, and correct the links of the ones that are not accepted yet.

Find your companies

Enter your roll number and the same email you used in the form.

Link of the specific job or HR email: the link of one job (not the home page, careers page or a search page), or an email. Fresher hiring link or email: the link of one specific job where freshers can apply, or an email. A careers page, home page or search page may not be approved in either field. You can fill either one or both.

Three details are compulsory for all five companies: the employee strength, the package offered to freshers (in LPA) and the yearly revenue of the company. Give a figure for each. Accepted companies are locked for links, but you can still fill these three details.
